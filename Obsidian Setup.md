## Tags
### Type

- **Concept** — Explains what something is and how it works.  
  *Example: PID Controller*

- **Method** — Explains how to perform or solve something.  
  *Example: Ziegler–Nichols PID Tuning*

- **Reference** — Information intended for quick lookup.  
  *Example: Laplace Transform Table*

- **Derivation** — Shows how something is mathematically or logically obtained.  
  *Example: Derivation of the State-Space Equations*

- **Application** — Shows how a concept is used in practice.  
  *Example: PID Control of a DC Motor*

- **Example** — Demonstrates a concept or method using a specific case.  
  *Example: Solving a Second-Order System*

- **Implementation** — Describes the actual realization in software or hardware.  
  *Example: Implementing a PID Controller on STM32*

### Status

- **Learning** — Currently studying or developing the note.
- **Complete** — The note is finished.
- **Verified** — The content has been checked and confirmed.

### Scope

- **Fundamental** — Basic principles and prerequisites.
- **Intermediate** — Builds on fundamentals with moderate complexity.
- **Advanced** — Complex, specialised, or highly technical material.
